The sales engine

The part most bots skip, plum actively sells. It recommends the right product, suggests the matching add-on, nudges the hesitant buyer, and rescues the cart before it goes cold, closing right there in the conversation. Your customer asks "do you have this in beige?" and leaves with the set and the matching covers.

Instant answers

Availability, shipping, returns, sizing, the same question for the hundredth time, answered in seconds, in your brand's voice, 24/7. Your customer messages at 1am and gets a real answer, not a "we'll get back to you." Tricky ones get handed to you at the right moment, nothing dropped.

Virtual try-on

She sees the piece on her own body before she buys, not on a model. She sends a photo, plum shows her the look, and the "how will this look on me?" hesitation that kills the sale goes away. She buys with confidence instead of clicking away to think about it.

Booking & orders

From "do you have a slot?" to a confirmed appointment or order, with instant confirmations and reminders that cut no-shows. No phone tag, no back-and-forth. Your customer books at midnight and wakes up to a confirmation; you wake up to the sale.

Order tracking

"Where's my order?" handled end to end. plum confirms, updates, and tracks every delivery, so those messages stop landing on you and your customers stay calm and informed, right up to "it's at your door."

Gets paid in chat

Close and collect in the same conversation. plum sends a secure payment link the moment a customer's ready, confirms it instantly, and never leaves money waiting on a follow-up. Less friction between "yes" and paid means more sales actually land.
