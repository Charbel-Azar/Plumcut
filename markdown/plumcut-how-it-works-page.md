# plumcut — "How it works" page (full copy)

*Ready-to-use page copy + light design notes. niche-agnostic. plum is the actor; no "AI agent / AI system" language. Lowercase plumcut / plum throughout.*

**Page job:** turn "done-for-you" from a claim into something the owner can picture and trust. Answer the quiet question behind every managed offer — *"what do I actually have to do, and what does it cost me in time?"* — and the answer is: almost nothing.

**SEO**
- Title: `How plumcut works — the AI that sells in your DMs, built and run for you`
- Description: `We learn your brand, build plum, and run it in your Instagram and WhatsApp DMs — answering, selling, and booking for you. Live in about two weeks. You operate nothing.`

---

## Section 1 — Hero / intro

**Eyebrow:** How it works

**H1:** plum does the talking. we do everything else.

**Sub:** You don't set anything up, learn any software, or manage a single conversation flow. We build plum around your brand, launch it in your DMs, and run it for you — so all you do is watch the sales and the insight come in.

**CTAs:** primary *"Talk to plum"* · secondary *"Book a 15-min call"*

*Design notes:* short hero, lots of air, plum mascot present. The promise to land instantly is "you operate nothing" — make that the emotional hook, not the process.

---

## Section 2 — The three steps (the spine of the page)

**H2:** From first hello to selling on its own — in three steps.

**Step 1 — We learn your brand**
We sit down with you once and learn how you talk, what you sell, your policies, your prices, and the questions you get asked a hundred times a week. That's the only real time you'll spend. Everything plum says afterward sounds like you, not a robot.

**Step 2 — We build & launch**
We build plum around what we learned and connect it to the channels your customers already use — Instagram, WhatsApp, your store. In about two weeks plum is live in your DMs, answering, selling, and booking. You don't touch a tool, write a flow, or configure anything.

**Step 3 — We run, sell & improve**
plum keeps working day and night. We watch it, fix what needs fixing, and sharpen how it sells as it learns your customers — week after week. You get the results, and a clear picture of what your customers actually want. It gets better the longer it runs.

*Design notes:* keep the existing 3-card visual language, restyled. Number them 01 / 02 / 03. Each card: bold step title, two-to-three sentence body, a small plum illustration. On mobile they stack; make step 1's "this is the only time you spend" stand out.

## Section 3 — What we do vs. what you do

**H2:** Here's the honest split.

**Two columns.**

**plum (and us) handle:**
- Answering every message, in your voice, 24/7
- Selling — recommending, upselling, rescuing carts, closing
- Booking appointments and taking orders
- Tracking orders and handling the "where's my package?" messages
- Reading every conversation and turning it into insight you can act on
- Monitoring, fixing, and improving all of it — continuously

**You handle:**
- Telling us about your brand, once
- Approving how plum sounds
- Reading the insights and deciding what to do with them

*Design notes:* two columns, clearly lopsided on purpose — your side is short, ours is long. That imbalance *is* the message. Use a subtle visual weight difference so the "you" column looks refreshingly light.

## Section 4 — Timeline

**H2:** Live in about two weeks.

A simple horizontal timeline:
- **Day 1 — Kickoff.** One conversation to learn your brand.
- **Days 2–10 — We build.** plum is trained on your voice, products, and policies, and connected to your channels.
- **Days 10–14 — Test & launch.** We test plum on real questions, you sign off, and it goes live in your DMs.
- **Ongoing — We run it.** Daily monitoring, fixes, and improvements. It gets sharper every week.

*Design notes:* horizontal stepper on desktop, vertical on mobile. Keep the language plain — no "phase 1 / sprint" jargon. The takeaway: fast, and you're barely involved.

## Section 8 — Quick reassurances (mini-FAQ)

**H2:** The questions everyone asks.

- **Do I have to operate anything?** No. We build plum, run it, and improve it. You read the results.
- **How long until it's live?** About two weeks from our first conversation.
- **Will it sound like a robot?** No — that's the whole point. plum is built around your voice and tone, and we keep tuning it.
- **What if a question is too tricky for plum?** plum hands it to you (or your team) at the right moment, with the full conversation, so nothing gets dropped.
- **What if my message volume suddenly spikes?** Your plan includes a generous amount of normal volume. If a campaign sends it way past that, there's a clear, fair rate — and we always flag you first. No surprise bills.
- **Can I start small and add more later?** Yes. Start with what you need most, add solutions as you grow.

*Design notes:* simple accordion or two-column list. End the section by routing the rest to plum: "More questions? Just ask plum — it's right there."
