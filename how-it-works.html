01

We learn your brand

We sit down with you once and learn how you talk, what you sell, your policies, your prices, and the questions you get asked a hundred times a week. That's the only real time you'll spend. Everything plum says afterward sounds like you, not a robot.

02

We build plum around what we learned and connect it to the channels your customers already use, Instagram, WhatsApp, your store. In about two weeks plum is live in your DMs, answering, selling, and booking. You don't touch a tool, write a flow, or configure anything.

plum keeps working day and night. We watch it, fix what needs fixing, and sharpen how it sells as it learns your customers, week after week. You get the results, and a clear picture of what your customers actually want. It gets better the longer it runs.

The questions everyone asks

Do I have to operate anything?

No. We build plum, run it, and improve it. You read the results.

How long until it's live?

About two weeks from our first conversation.

Will it sound like a robot?

No, that's the whole point. plum is built around your voice and tone, and we keep tuning it.

What if a question is too tricky for plum?

plum hands it to you (or your team) at the right moment, with the full conversation, so nothing gets dropped.

What if my message volume suddenly spikes?

Your plan includes a generous amount of normal volume. If a campaign sends it way past that, there's a clear, fair rate, and we always flag you first. No surprise bills.

Can I start small and add more later?

Yes. Start with what you need most, add solutions as you grow.
